MOS provides atomic-level non-covalent interaction analysis to help you better understand protein structures.

You can inspect interactions across multiple residues. MOS currently supports three types: "H-Bond", "π-π Stack", and "π-Cation".

It also includes "Hotspots", which ranks positions in descending order by interaction count to help you quickly identify key sites.
