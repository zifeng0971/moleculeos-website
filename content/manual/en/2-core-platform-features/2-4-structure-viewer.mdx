When one or more proteins are selected in the left protein Structure Tree area, the corresponding protein structure will be displayed below the sequence area on the right, which is the structure area.

The lower-right panel renders the 3D molecular coordinates of selected proteins. Residue selection remains fully synchronized with the sequence panel.
