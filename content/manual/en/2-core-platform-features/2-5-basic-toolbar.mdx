<h4 id="2-core-platform-features-2-5-basic-toolbar-method-1-global-representation-change"><a id="heading_44"></a><strong>Method 1: Global Representation Change</strong></h4>

-   <strong>Target Selection:</strong> In the left protein tree, expand the protein node and select the target chain or model.
-   <strong>Invoke function:</strong> Hover over the icon next to the node to reveal the options menu.
-   <strong>Select Style:</strong> Choose from Cartoon, Ball and Stick, or Surface to apply the rendering style globally.

<h4 id="2-core-platform-features-2-5-basic-toolbar-method-2-local-representation-change"><a id="heading_45"></a><strong>Method 2: Local Representation Change</strong></h4>

-   <strong>Target Selection:</strong> Select specific residues in the structure or sequence view (multi-selection supported).
-   <strong>Select Style:</strong> Click the desired style button on the right toolbar to update only the selected residues.

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-5-basic-toolbar-residue-distance-measurement"><a id="heading_46"></a><strong>Residue Distance Measurement</strong></h3>

MOS supports the measurement <strong>of the distance between two residues</strong>, which is measured in Å with a precision of two decimal places.

-   <strong>Select Residues:</strong> Select two target residues in the structure or sequence viewer.
-   <strong>Execute:</strong> Click the Distance tool on the toolbar to display the calculated measurement.
