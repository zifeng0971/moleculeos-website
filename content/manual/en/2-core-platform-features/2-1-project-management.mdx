<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-1-project-management-project-concept"><a id="heading_24"></a><strong>Project Concept</strong></h3>

Projects in MoleculeOS serve as dedicated workspaces for specific protein engineering campaigns. Users can organize, track, and share all associated proteins, computational tasks, and analysis pipelines within a single secure environment.

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-1-project-management-viewing-the-project-list"><a id="heading_25"></a><strong>Viewing the Project List</strong></h3>

Click the dropdown menu next to the current project name in the top navigation bar to view all accessible projects.

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-1-project-management-creating-a-project"><a id="heading_26"></a><strong>Creating a Project</strong></h3>

Open the project list, click "Create Project", provide a project title and description, and click "Submit". The creator is designated as the Project Owner.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-1-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-1-project-management-switching-projects"><a id="heading_27"></a><strong>Switching Projects</strong></h3>

Open the project dropdown menu and click on the target project name to switch workspaces.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-4-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-1-project-management-editing-project-metadata"><a id="heading_28"></a><strong>Editing Project Metadata</strong></h3>

Project Owners and Editors have permission to modify project settings. Hover over a project entry in the list, click the edit icon, update the name or description, and click "Submit" to save changes.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-5-poster.webp"} width={2040} height={1016} alt={""} />

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-1-project-management-deleting-a-project"><a id="heading_29"></a><strong>Deleting a Project</strong></h3>

Project Owners and Editors can delete non-default projects. Hover over the target project in the list, click the delete icon, and confirm the prompt.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-6-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-1-project-management-project-sharing"><a id="heading_30"></a><strong>Project Sharing</strong></h3>

Project Owners and Project Editors have the permission to share projects via:

-   <strong>Option 1 - Copy Link:</strong> Click "Share" in the top navigation bar, select "Share link", and copy the URL.
-   <strong>Option 2 - Member Invitations:</strong> Add team members directly by user handle or email.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-7-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

Grant specific users access to this project by adding project members. For detailed operation steps, please refer to the section below.

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-1-project-management-project-member-management"><a id="heading_31"></a><strong>Project Member Management</strong></h3>

-   <strong>Adding Members:</strong> Click <strong>Share</strong>, search for colleagues within your organization, assign their permission level (<strong>Read</strong> or <strong>Edit</strong>), and click <strong>Invite</strong>.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-8-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

-   <strong>Removing Members:</strong> Open the member list in the Share panel and click the remove icon next to the relevant user.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-9-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

-   <strong>Modifying Permissions:</strong> Adjust access levels directly using the permission dropdown selector next to each member's name.
