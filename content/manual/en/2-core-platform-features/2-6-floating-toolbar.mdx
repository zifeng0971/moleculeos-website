Positioned at the bottom of the screen, the floating toolbar houses core analytical capabilities: structural superimposition, model execution, homology search, and Mocha AI.

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-6-floating-toolbar-structural-superimposition"><a id="heading_49"></a><strong>Structural Superimposition</strong></h3>

Aligns multiple protein structures to compare structural variations and binding site conformations.

-   <strong>Select & Launch:</strong> Select the candidate proteins in the left node tree, then click "Superimpose" on the bottom toolbar.

<ManualVideo src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-23.webm"} poster={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-23-poster.webp"} webm={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-23.webm"} mp4={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-23.mp4"} external={false} reference={"image42.gif"} />

-   <strong>Select Proteins:</strong> Check two or more structures in the selection modal.
-   <strong>Select Reference Structure:</strong>
-   "Fixed" designates the static reference structure against which all other structures align. Click the "Fixed" tag to toggle it to "Moving", and vice versa.
-   Click the "Fixed" tag to switch it to "Moving". Click "Moving" to switch it back to "Fixed".
-   A group can only have one reference protein; all other proteins are automatically set to "Moving".
-   <strong>Select chains to align:</strong> Because a protein may contain multiple chains, choose which chain to use for alignment across all proteins.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-24-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

-   <strong>Confirm:</strong> Click "Confirm" to align the selected proteins.

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-6-floating-toolbar-residue-search-5a"><a id="heading_51"></a><strong>Residue Search (5Å)</strong></h3>

After selecting one or more residues, you can filter for surrounding residues based on distance and type. Use this feature to save key sites to your project so other members can view them.

<em>Only project Owners and Editors can create, edit, and delete. Viewers can only view.</em>

-   <strong>Select residues:</strong> Select one or multiple target residues in the structure or sequence view.

<ManualVideo src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-37.webm"} poster={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-37-poster.webp"} webm={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-37.webm"} mp4={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-37.mp4"} external={false} reference={"image56.gif"} />

-   <strong>Open the tool:</strong> Click "5Å" on the bottom toolbar.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-38-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

-   <strong>Set filter criteria (the tool takes the intersection of both conditions):</strong>
-   <strong>Distance:</strong> Filters residues within a defined radius around the selected amino acids. Supports a range of 1 Å to 10 Å with 0.1 Å precision. Use the up/down arrows for 0.5 Å increments, or type a value directly.
-   <strong>Residue Type:</strong> Select specific amino acid types to narrow down the selection.
-   <strong>Name and confirm:</strong> Once filtered, click "Next", enter a name and description, and click "Confirm". The group will be saved under "Amino acid group" on the left.
-   <strong>View / Copy / Delete:</strong> Follow the same steps as Annotation.
