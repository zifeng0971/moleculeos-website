<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-2-protein-management-adding-proteins"><a id="heading_33"></a><strong>Adding Proteins</strong></h3>

<h4 id="2-core-platform-features-2-2-protein-management-empty-project"><a id="heading_34"></a><strong>Empty Project</strong></h4>

Click the upload target in the center of the canvas and select a valid local file (.pdb, .cif).

<h4 id="2-core-platform-features-2-2-protein-management-project-with-existing-proteins"><a id="heading_35"></a><strong>Project with existing proteins</strong></h4>

Click "Add new" at the top of the left protein node tree and upload additional files.

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-2-protein-management-deleting-proteins"><a id="heading_36"></a><strong>Deleting Proteins</strong></h3>

Hover over the target protein in the left node tree, click the "More" menu (three dots), select "Delete", and confirm.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/video/video-13-poster.webp"} width={1952} height={1062} alt={""} />

<h3 id="2-core-platform-features-2-2-protein-management-extracting-cropping-chains"><a id="heading_37"></a><strong>Extracting &amp; Cropping Chains</strong></h3>

Hover over a protein node, click the scissor/crop icon, select the desired sub-chains or domains in the hierarchy, and click "Load selected structures" to import the isolated chains into the project.
