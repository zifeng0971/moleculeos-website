When one or more proteins are selected in the left node tree, their full primary sequences appear in the upper-right sequence panel.

-   <strong>Resizing:</strong> Drag the lower boundary line of the sequence panel to adjust vertical height.
-   <strong>Bidirectional Highlighting:</strong> Clicking any residue in the sequence panel automatically highlights the corresponding site in the 3D structure viewer, and vice versa.
