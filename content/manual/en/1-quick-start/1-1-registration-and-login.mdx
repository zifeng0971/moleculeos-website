<h3 id="1-quick-start-1-1-registration-and-login-new-user-registration"><a id="heading_15"></a><strong>New User Registration</strong></h3>

URL: https://mos.moleculemind.com/

<h4 id="1-quick-start-1-1-registration-and-login-individual-users"><a id="heading_16"></a><strong>Individual Users</strong></h4>

The platform supports open registration for individual accounts using a valid email address.

<strong>Step 1:</strong> Enter your email address and accept the User Agreement and Privacy Policy.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/img/image-002.webp"} width={3022} height={1640} alt={""} />

  

<strong>Step 2:</strong> Enter the verification code received via email and click Next.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/img/image-003.webp"} width={3024} height={1636} alt={""} />

<strong>Step 3:</strong> Set your password and complete your profile information.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/img/image-004.webp"} width={3024} height={1645} alt={""} />

<strong>Step 4:</strong> Account creation complete.

Click Get Started to access the platform.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/img/image-005.webp"} width={3024} height={1645} alt={""} />

<h4 id="1-quick-start-1-1-registration-and-login-enterprise-access-requests"><a id="heading_17"></a><strong>Enterprise Access Requests</strong></h4>

Advanced permissions are available for enterprise organizations. To request enterprise onboarding, send an inquiry to [<strong>mos@moleculemind.com</strong>](mailto:mos@moleculemind.com). Access tiers will be provisioned following verification.

<ManualImage src={"/manual-assets/en/img/image-006.webp"} width={3024} height={1646} alt={""} />

<h3 id="1-quick-start-1-1-registration-and-login-login"><a id="heading_18"></a><strong>Login</strong></h3>

-   <strong>Individual Users:</strong> Log in using your registered username/email and password.
-   <strong>Enterprise Users:</strong> Log in using the enterprise identifier, username, and password assigned by your administrator.
