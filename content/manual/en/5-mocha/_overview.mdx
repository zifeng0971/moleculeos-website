<h3 id="5-mocha-overview-result-output"><a id="heading_68"></a><strong>Result Output</strong></h3>

Once all tasks are complete, Mocha outputs an analysis report that typically includes:

-   <strong>Structure prediction:</strong> Displays molecular composition, sequence length, predicted structure, and quality metrics.
-   <strong>Structure and functional analysis:</strong> Details key regions, binding interfaces, interactions, and critical residues.
-   <strong>Candidate mutation recommendations:</strong> Lists suggested mutations, positions, scores, and rationales.
-   <strong>Summary:</strong> Summarizes key findings and provides recommendations for downstream screening or validation.
-   <strong>Result files:</strong> Generates reports, data tables, and structural coordinate files for online viewing or download.
