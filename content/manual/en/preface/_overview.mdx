<h3 id="preface-overview-what-can-moleculeos-do-for-you"><a id="heading_2"></a><strong>What can MoleculeOS do for you?</strong></h3>

Without writing scripts, you can issue tasks directly in natural language via the chat interface:

<strong>Molecular Computing & Intelligent Design</strong>

-   <strong>Structure Prediction:</strong> Input sequences to automatically predict 3D structures of proteins, antibodies, or complexes, assess regional confidence metrics, and generate multiple models for evaluation.
-   <strong>Mutation Effect Prediction:</strong> Run four complementary scoring dimensions in a single step—sequence fitness, evolutionary conservation, structural folding stability, and binding affinity. Cross-filter robust variants across multiple parameters to prevent single-metric bias.
-   <strong>Antibody Humanization (Beta):</strong> Input antibody sequences to automatically execute the humanization pipeline and output ranked candidate sequences that retain antigen-binding capability.
-   <strong>Homology Search & 3D Visualization:</strong> Identify distant homolog proteins and analyze evolutionary relationships. Highlight specific residues, measure distances, and customize display representations in the 3D molecular viewer for clear structural insights.

<strong>Expert Antibody Engineering Workflows</strong>

-   <strong>Binding Mode Analysis:</strong> Intelligently identify heavy chains, light chains, and antigens within complex structures. Perform deep-dive analyses of interface interactions (hydrogen bonds, salt bridges, hydrophobic contacts, π-π stacking, etc.) and generate structured reports with biological interpretations.
-   <strong>Affinity Optimization & Developability Assessment:</strong> Score sequences across sequence, structure, evolution, and binding free energy in parallel to locate high-potential mutation sites. Automatically scan CDR regions for liability motifs (e.g., glycosylation, deamidation, oxidation) to mitigate downstream process and stability risks early.

<strong>Rational Enzyme Engineering</strong>

-   <strong>Catalytic Mechanism & Activity Optimization:</strong> Identify active-site residues and analyze catalytic triads or cofactor-binding modes. Recommend substitution strategies to enhance catalytic efficiency based on pocket geometry and sequence conservation.
-   <strong>Stability Enhancement & Conformational Analysis:</strong> Differentiate active-site residues from scaffold positions to propose precise thermostability mutations (e.g., disulfide engineering, hydrophobic core optimization) without impairing catalytic function. Compare substrate-bound and apo states to track critical loop dynamics.

<strong>Target Research & Construct Design</strong>

-   <strong>Target Landscape Research:</strong> Input target names or gene symbols to automatically aggregate UniProt annotations, PDB structures, and literature into a comprehensive report.
-   <strong>Expression Construct Design:</strong> Identify topological features such as signal peptides, transmembrane regions, and domain boundaries to recommend optimal expression fragments, affinity tags, and host systems for structural or functional assays.

<h3 id="preface-overview-how-does-it-work"><a id="heading_3"></a><strong>How Does It Work?</strong></h3>

MoleculeOS is more than a simple computation portal—it operates as an AI collaborator equipped with scientific reasoning:

-   <strong>Research First, Compute Second:</strong> Before running heavy computations, the system analyzes biological context using literature and curated databases to avoid unstructured calculation runs.
-   <strong>Autonomous Planning & Parallel Execution:</strong> Complex objectives are decomposed into structured workflows. Long-running computational tasks execute in the background via multithreading without blocking ongoing user interaction.
-   <strong>Cross-Validation & Transparent Reporting:</strong> Results are cross-referenced with established biological principles and flagged if discrepancies arise. Missing data or assumption-based inferences are explicitly documented.

<h3 id="preface-overview-target-audience"><a id="heading_4"></a><strong>Target Audience</strong></h3>

-   <strong>Wet-Lab Scientists (Antibody, Protein, and Enzyme Engineers):</strong> Gain structure-based rational design suggestions and multi-dimensional scoring support for humanization, affinity maturation, thermostability optimization, or catalytic mechanism studies.
-   <strong>Dry-Lab Specialists (Computational Biologists & Protein Designers):</strong> Automate repetitive scripting and data-wrangling pipelines, delegating workflow execution to focus on higher-level scientific evaluation.
