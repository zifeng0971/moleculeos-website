Crystal structure of human PD-L1 (programmed death ligand 1) in complex with a VHH nanobody at 1.7 Å resolution. PD-L1 is an immune checkpoint protein expressed on various cells that interacts with PD-1 and plays a role in immune regulation. The nanobody binds the PD-L1 extracellular region, making this complex useful for understanding PD-L1 recognition by single-domain antibodies. No non-solvent small-molecule ligand interaction is indicated.
ReasoningThe structure contains a single protein antigen chain (entity 1, likely PD-L1) bound to a VHH nanobody chain (entity 2), as indicated by the PDB title, antibody fragment annotation, antibody chains, antigen chains, and a valid interface with 51 contacts. This is a direct antigen-antibody complex, not an enzyme, receptor-ligand signalling complex, nucleic acid complex, or simple monomer.
